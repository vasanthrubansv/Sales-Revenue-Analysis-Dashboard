# Power BI Dashboard Design

## Page 1 — Sales Overview

Top KPI cards:
1. Total Sales
2. Total Revenue
3. Total Orders
4. Units Sold
5. Average Order Value

Main visuals:
- Line chart: Monthly Revenue Trend
- Bar chart: Sales by Category
- Horizontal bar chart: Top 10 Products by Revenue
- Donut chart: Revenue by Payment Method
- Column chart: Revenue by Region

Slicers:
- Order Date
- Region
- Category
- Product
- Payment Method
